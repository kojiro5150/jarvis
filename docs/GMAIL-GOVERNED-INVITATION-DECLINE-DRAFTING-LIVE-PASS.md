# Gmail governed invitation-decline drafting — live pass

**Status:** **LIVE PASS / capability-specific** on 11 September 2026.

## Exact promoted capability

JARVIS may prepare one proposed thank-and-decline reply to a professional-network invitation when:

- one exact eligible Gmail private-release reference is current;
- the user's request matches the closed invitation-decline purpose;
- the user grants fresh one-shot authority to re-read that exact message;
- the complete policy-processed evidence fits within the frozen 16,000-code-unit admission bound; and
- the returned draft passes the deterministic integrity checks.

This is purpose-bounded semantic composition over governed private Gmail evidence. It is not a Gmail provider write.

## Live production proof

The live governed-console sequence used the real Gmail connector:

1. Search for the user's latest Raman Bhola email under explicit Gmail-search authority.
2. Select the second bounded result, a LinkedIn invitation from Raman Bhola with subject `I want to connect`.
3. Read that exact message under separate explicit Gmail-read authority.
4. Request: `Draft a reply to Raman, saying thank you for the invite but politely decline.`
5. Grant fresh one-shot authority to re-read that exact message for the drafting purpose.
6. Receive a grounded proposed reply that thanked Raman and politely declined.

The result:

- retained the exact server-owned sender and subject;
- was grounded in the freshly acquired invitation;
- contained no lunch, Thursday meeting or other fabricated invitation detail;
- stated `This message has not been sent.`; and
- created no Gmail draft and performed no Gmail provider mutation.

Follow-up requests `Shorten that draft.` and `Send it.` both returned the fixed containment response. No revision, reuse or send occurred.

## Promotion boundary

The following remain unimplemented and unauthorised:

- general-purpose Gmail drafting;
- summarisation through this drafting path;
- editing or reusing the generated proposal;
- saving the proposal as a Gmail draft;
- sending a reply;
- any other Gmail mailbox mutation; and
- standing authority derived from the successful read or composition.

The architectural pattern may be reused by later capabilities, but this proof may not be inherited by them.
